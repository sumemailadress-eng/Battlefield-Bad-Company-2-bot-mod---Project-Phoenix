How to

Mouse: left-drag orbit, right-drag pan, wheel zoom. WASD move, Q/E down/up, Shift faster, hold the middle button to look around. T top view.
Select / move: click a route line to select it. Click a white waypoint to pick it, then click the ground to move it there. Alt+click the ground inserts a waypoint after the picked one.
Water exits: click the ground at a ramp or stairs out of the water to add one, click an exit to remove it. Swimming bots head for the nearest one within 150 m.
Routes out of an HQ or parking lot: the coloured boxes are the vehicles. Pick "Vehicle route" below, click Draw new and start your route right beside a vehicle.
Draw new: click the ground to add waypoints, Enter finishes. Use "Hide above" to see into buildings and click lower floors.
Branch from here: pick a waypoint, press it, draw the new end - a new route that shares the start.
Del deletes the picked waypoint, Ctrl+Z undoes, Esc cancels.
Foot routes lead soldiers to an objective: end one at a flag or M-COM. Vehicle routes lead drivers: start one beside the vehicles. Bots stay on them to the end.
Saving writes navcache\\<map>.froutes / .vroutes (old copies go to navcache\\backup_3d). The running bots pick them up within 5 s.
